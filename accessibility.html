Accessibility Statement

Bravura Detailing is committed to providing quality service and clear communication to all customers. Our business operates out of a private home garage, which currently does not offer handicap-accessible features such as wheelchair ramps, designated accessible parking, or ADA-compliant entryways.

Facility Accessibility

Due to the residential nature of our location, the workspace may have limited accessibility, including uneven surfaces, steps, or narrow access points that may not be suitable for individuals with mobility challenges.

Accommodations & Alternatives

While our physical location has limitations, we are committed to working with all customers to provide reasonable accommodations whenever possible. This may include:

  • —Communicating via phone, text, or online to handle booking and questions
  • —Arranging alternative service options when feasible (such as mobile service, if offered)
  • —Coordinating drop-off and pickup arrangements to minimize inconvenience

Communication Accessibility

We strive to ensure all customers can easily communicate with us regarding services, pricing, and scheduling. If you have specific accessibility needs, we encourage you to contact us in advance so we can do our best to assist.

Ongoing Commitment

We understand the importance of accessibility and will continue to evaluate ways to improve our services and customer experience within the limitations of our current setup.

If you have any questions or require accommodations, please contact Bravura Detailing prior to your appointment so we can discuss available options.

By scheduling a service, you acknowledge and understand the accessibility limitations of our location.
