Refund Policy

At Bravura Detailing, we take pride in delivering high-quality detailing services with a strong focus on consistency, professionalism, and customer satisfaction at the time your vehicle is completed and returned to you. Because detailing is a labor-intensive, customized service that varies by vehicle condition, we have established the following policy to ensure clarity and fairness for all customers.

All Sales Are Final

All services provided by Bravura Detailing are considered final sale once completed. Due to the time, labor, products, and materials involved in each detail, we do not offer refunds, partial refunds, or service credits after the work has been performed. Each vehicle is unique, and results are based on its condition at the time of service, which is why all purchases are non-refundable.

Deposits

Deposits required to secure certain services (such as paint correction or ceramic coating) are non-refundable. However, a deposit can be applied toward rescheduling your service to another date or time. Deposits are forfeited in the event of a late cancellation or no-show without sufficient notice.

Customer Inspection Required

We require every customer to inspect their vehicle thoroughly at the time of pickup before leaving the premises. This step ensures that you have the opportunity to review the results, ask questions, and confirm that the service meets your expectations.

If you notice any areas of concern, missed spots, or have specific requests, they must be brought to our attention immediately during pickup so we can address them on the spot. We are committed to making reasonable touch-ups when notified at that time.

Once the vehicle has been picked up and leaves our care, the service is considered fully accepted and completed, and no refunds, re-dos, or adjustments will be provided after departure.

No Refunds Due to Weather

Bravura Detailing is not responsible for any environmental or weather-related conditions that may affect your vehicle after the service has been completed. This includes, but is not limited to, rain, wind, dust, pollen, road debris, or other natural elements that can impact the cleanliness or appearance of your vehicle.

Because these conditions are outside of our control, no refunds, discounts, or re-services will be issued due to weather or environmental exposure after your appointment has concluded. We recommend monitoring the weather forecast and planning your appointment accordingly for the best results.

Pricing & Service Condition Policy

At Bravura Detailing, all service prices provided prior to your appointment are considered estimates based on standard vehicle conditions. Because every vehicle varies in cleanliness, wear, and overall condition, final pricing may change once the vehicle is physically inspected in person.

Estimates Are Subject to Change

Quoted prices — whether provided online, by phone, or in person — are starting estimates only. These estimates are based on vehicles in average condition and do not account for excessive buildup, neglect, or specialty situations. Final pricing will be confirmed after a full visual inspection at the time of your appointment.

Vehicle Condition Impacts Pricing

Additional charges may apply if the vehicle requires extra time, labor, tools, or specialized treatment beyond a standard detail. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • —Excessive trash, clutter, or personal belongings requiring removal
  • —Heavy or embedded pet hair in carpets, seats, or trunk areas
  • —Severe stains from food, drinks, grease, ink, or unknown substances
  • —Strong or persistent odors (smoke, pet, mildew, etc.) requiring treatment
  • —Excessive dirt, mud, sand, or debris buildup
  • —Spills that have soaked into upholstery or carpeting
  • —Mold, mildew, or biohazard-related contamination (service will be refused at the time of appointment and any payment refunded — this falls outside the scope of what we service, and the customer will need to find another provider)
  • —Bodily fluids (vomit, urine, etc.) requiring specialized cleaning (service will be refused at the time of appointment and any payment refunded — this falls outside the scope of what we service, and the customer will need to find another provider)
  • —Excessive bugs, tar, or sap on exterior surfaces
  • —Neglected paint requiring additional decontamination time
  • —Oxidation, heavy water spots, or hard mineral buildup
  • —Overspray, tree sap, or environmental fallout removal
  • —Vehicles that have not been cleaned for extended periods
  • —Large vehicles or vehicles with complex interiors (third rows, tight crevices, etc.)
  • —Aftermarket modifications that require extra care or time
  • —Excessive brake dust or heavily soiled wheels and wheel wells
  • —Sand or debris from beach or off-road use
  • —Interior surfaces with heavy buildup of oils, residue, or grime

These conditions can significantly increase the time and effort required to properly complete the service and may result in an adjusted final price.

Approval Before Additional Charges

If your vehicle requires additional work beyond the original estimate, we will communicate any pricing adjustments before proceeding. No additional charges will be applied without your knowledge and approval.

Our Commitment

We are committed to transparent, fair pricing and delivering high-quality results with every service. Before any work begins, we ensure you fully understand the scope of work and the final cost so there are no surprises.

While all services are final sale, our goal is to provide a positive experience and results you're satisfied with. We encourage open communication and a thorough inspection at pickup to ensure your vehicle meets your expectations before you leave.

By scheduling a service with Bravura Detailing, you acknowledge and agree that all initial quotes are estimates, that final pricing is subject to change based on the condition of the vehicle, and that deposits are non-refundable but may be applied toward rescheduling your service to another date or time.
